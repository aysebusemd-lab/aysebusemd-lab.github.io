Mortal Balance (CrazyGames x Construct Game Jam Entry) | 2D Character Art | Game UI Design

#Procreate

Mortal Balance is a narrative-driven 2D game about the damage that comes with having too much power. The player takes the role of Insik, Protector of Life, in a conflict with Samara, Guider of the Dead. Both believe they protect humanity, but their attacks endanger the mortal world beneath them. Created in a team of two for the CrazyGames × Construct Game Jam.

Challenge

The jam’s theme, “Overpowered,” raises a question: what happens when winning a fight means harming the people being protected? The story needs to give both characters a reason to believe they are right. Insik sees life as a gift that deserves protection, including its hardships. Samara sees prolonged suffering as cruelty and takes souls early out of mercy. Their disagreement needs to carry through the dialogue, character designs and player choices, so the consequences feel connected to their beliefs.

Process

Character profiles establish each figure’s motives, loyalties and contradictions before the script develops their confrontation. Insik’s devotion to divine order makes him protective but unwilling to question authority. Samara’s empathy drives her rebellion, yet she assumes the right to decide when another person’s life should end. The dialogue draws on these tensions, with each character challenging the other’s idea of mercy.

A paper storyboard maps the opening, arena view, alternating dialogue, choices, attacks and ending. The script develops this structure into five rounds with different responses and consequences. Two resources, Mortal Balance and Clash Pressure, connect the decisions to the outcome: excessive force damages the world, while too much restraint leaves Samara free to continue. The character portraits, gameplay sprites and animations are hand-drawn in Procreate. Insik’s white wings, gold accessories and warm colours draw on angelic imagery. Samara’s hood, cool skin and dark purple clothing give her a contrasting silhouette. Their dialogue frames continue these differences through gold ornament and wing details for Insik, and purple borders with branch-like decoration for Samara.

Adobe Illustrator was used to bring together the dialogue boxes, nameplates, choice buttons and resource display. Programming, sound design and integration in Construct 3 was handled by my teammate.

Solution

Mortal Balance makes restraint part of the fight. The player needs enough force to confront Samara while preserving the world Insik claims to defend. Branching dialogue and multiple endings show the cost of that balance, including a hollow victory where defeating an opponent undermines the purpose of fighting. The character art gives the conflict two distinct faces, while the writing leaves room to question both of them.
