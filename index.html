    College Student Bedroom│3D Modeling│Environmental & Prop Design

    A 3D bedroom scene modeled in Blender, inspired by a combination of my university dorm and my old room back home. The project began as a collection of studies of everyday objects and gradually developed into a complete interior over the course of > 5 months.

    3D Showcase

    Wingardium Laviosa in LEGO | Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone | 3D Animation

    A short, LEGO-style Harry Potter cinematic created using Blender, Adobe Illustrator, and Unity. I worked with existing LEGO models from Mecabricks to build a classroom in Hogwarts, designed custom character textures, and brought everything together through animation, lighting, and camera work on Unity.

    View Case Study
    Progress Vlog

    Piece by Piece (Eyeshadow Palette) | 3D Product Design | The Amazing Digital Circus Fan Art

    A fan-made eyeshadow palette inspired by Zooble from The Amazing Digital Circus, combining vector illustration, packaging design, and 3D modeling. Every graphic element was created manually in Adobe Illustrator, assembled into packaging artwork in Photoshop, and applied to a palette modeled in Blender.

    View Case Study

    Paranoia [ DEMO ] │Game Design│Prototype Development

    Paranoia is a first-person psychological horror game set in a late-1990s supermarket. Inspired by a true story, the game follows Ashley, a young cashier, whose ordinary evening takes a disturbing turn. With a focus on anxiety, social pressure, and unreliable perception, the game explores the uncertainty of whether danger is real or a product of fear.

    View Case Study
    Playable Demo

    AURA Lamp Advertisement Scene | 3D Modeling | Environmental Design

    A product design and 3D visualization project that started with a simple paper cube and developed into Aura Light, a conceptual mood lamp. The project combines physical prototyping, branding, environmental modelling, and rendering, with the final advertisement presented in a realistic 3D bus stop scene.

    View Case Study
