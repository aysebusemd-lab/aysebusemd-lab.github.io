AURA Lamp Advertisement Scene | 3D Modeling | Environmental Design

#Blender#Photoshop

A product design and 3D visualization project that started with a simple paper cube and developed into Aura Light, a conceptual mood lamp. The project combines physical prototyping, branding, environmental modelling, and rendering, with the final advertisement presented in a realistic 3D bus stop scene.

Challenge

The assignment introduced the fundamentals of prototyping by combining physical construction with digital 3D modelling. The initial task was to build a precise 4 × 4 × 4 cm cube from a single sheet of A4 paper, then explore how the same geometry could be recreated digitally and converted back into a printable paper model. The project was expanded into a product design and advertising exercise, requiring an original concept, promotional material, and a realistic 3D setting in which to present it. This led to Aura Light, a minimalist, paper-inspired mood lamp designed to switch between cold, neutral, and warm lighting. The main challenge was to develop a consistent visual identity while connecting physical prototyping, product design, and environmental visualization into one project.

Process

The physical prototype began with a hand-drawn net of six connected squares and additional folding tabs. The paper was cut and temporarily assembled with tape to check the proportions before the edges were permanently glued. For the product's branding, an A3 poster was designed in Canva using a sage green and beige colour palette, organic swirl patterns, repeating typography, and hand-drawn illustrations. Black outlines and layered paper textures were used to give the design a handcrafted appearance. The advertisement was then placed in a bus stop environment modelled in Blender. Particular attention went into making the scene feel like a believable public space, from separate material transitions between the pavement and road to raised tactile guidance strips that interact naturally with the lighting. Dirt, rust, and weathering were added around stone joints and metal supports, while overhanging vegetation helped balance the industrial structure with its surroundings. An Antwerp transit map, schedule signage, glass panels, and a trash bin were included to complete the setting. Materials, shadows, and camera composition were refined to give the final render a realistic appearance. To complete the prototyping cycle, Blender's Export Paper Model add-on was used to unfold the digital cube into a printable template. The pattern was then printed, cut, folded, and assembled, with careful attention to edge alignment and folding accuracy.

Solution

The finished work consists of a physical cube prototype, a conceptual mood lamp, an A3 promotional poster, a realistic Blender advertisement render, and a reconstructed paper model exported from the digital version. The final advertisement presents Aura Light as a product that could naturally appear in an everyday urban setting, with the campaign's visual identity carried through from the product illustration to the billboard. The detailed environment provides a convincing context for the design rather than displaying it as an isolated mockup. The reconstructed paper model completes the physical-to-digital-to-physical workflow, demonstrating how Blender geometry can be translated into a printable object while maintaining its intended proportions. Together, the deliverables turn a basic construction exercise into a product concept with its own branding, physical prototypes, and advertising presentation.
