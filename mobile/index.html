Piece by Piece (Eyeshadow Palette) | 3D Product Design | The Amazing Digital Circus Fan Art

A fan-made eyeshadow palette inspired by Zooble from The Amazing Digital Circus, combining vector illustration, packaging design, and 3D modeling. Every graphic element was created manually in Adobe Illustrator, assembled into packaging artwork in Photoshop, and applied to a palette modeled in Blender.

Paranoia [ DEMO ] │Game Design│Prototype Development

Paranoia is a first-person psychological horror game set in a late-1990s supermarket. Inspired by a true story, the game follows Ashley, a young cashier, whose ordinary evening takes a disturbing turn. With a focus on anxiety, social pressure, and unreliable perception, the game explores the uncertainty of whether danger is real or a product of fear.

A product design and 3D visualization project that started with a simple paper cube and developed into Aura Light, a conceptual mood lamp.
