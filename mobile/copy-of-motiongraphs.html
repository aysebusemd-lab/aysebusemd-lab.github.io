3D Visual Effects (VFX) | Live-Action Footage Manipulation | 3D Camera Tracking & Compositing

#Blender#AfterEffects

A VFX project addressing five challenges within a single moving tabletop shot: adding a reflective metal sphere, removing unwanted details, replacing a framed picture with glass reflections, integrating metal rings around an existing object, and introducing an original addition. The project combines Blender, Mocha AE and After Effects for 3D rendering, footage cleanup and image replacement.

Challenge

The assignment required five different interventions to remain consistent within the same camera move. A metal ball needed to sit on the central black cage; the hole on the left side of the table and the tracking markers required removal; the framed picture needed a replacement that preserved reflective glass and foreground overlaps; two metal rings needed to fit around the stacked cylinders on the right; and an additional creative element needed to extend the scene. Together, these tasks required control over perspective, reflections, shadows and occlusion.

Process

For the metal ball, the Blender scene uses a tracked camera and aligned reference geometry to position the sphere above the cage. Scratched-aluminium textures control its colour, roughness and surface detail, while an HDRI provides environmental reflections. Shadow-catcher geometry supports its integration with the filmed surfaces. The tabletop cleanup uses fill sequences and paint elements in After Effects to replace unwanted areas with surrounding surface detail. These patches address the hole and tracking markers while following the moving shot. For the picture replacement, Mocha AE supplies a planar track of the frame, and Corner Pin effects fit the “Turkey Mentioned” image to its changing perspective. A separate reflection layer preserves the appearance of glass over the inserted image, while foreground masking maintains the overlaps of objects in front of the frame. The rings use torus geometry in Blender and sit around the stacked cylinders. Reflective shading connects them visually to the sphere, while shadow-catcher geometry represents the existing cylindrical surfaces. The open-ended fifth challenge takes the form of a cigarette-themed prop arrangement. A textured cigarette pack, an ashtray and cigarettes occupy the tabletop, introducing additional shapes and material finishes.

Solution

The final shot places the metal sphere on the black cage at the centre of the table, replaces the framed picture with a Turkey meme, and adds a cigarette pack, ashtray and cigarette to the foreground arrangement. Across the camera move, the additions occupy distinct positions within the scene: the sphere acts as the central reflective element, the picture provides a personalised detail, and the smaller props extend the tabletop composition. The completed sequence is presented as a 3840 × 2160 video at 25 frames per second.
