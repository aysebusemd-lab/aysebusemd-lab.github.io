WanderWood - Indie Music Festival | Brand Identity | Graphic Design

#Photoshop

Wanderwood is an outdoor music festival concept bringing folk, soft indie and alternative music into a woodland setting. Its graphics combine forest photography, newspaper clippings and mismatched cutout letters, taking inspiration from scrapbooks and memories of camping trips. The poster, social posts and merchandise share an earthy, handmade look that reflects the festival’s relaxed atmosphere.

Challenge

The challenge is to capture the feeling of spending a few days outdoors with music, friends and room to wander. The graphics need to feel playful and loosely assembled while keeping the lineup, dates and location easy to find. Each format also requires a different arrangement: a poster holds the full event announcement, social banners introduce the festival, and the Instagram carousel gives more space to the artists.

Process

The visual research brings together camping photographs, forest landscapes, vintage posters, botanical collages and old paper textures. Deep greens and warm orange sit alongside cream, mustard and dark blue, drawing colour from the woods and the materials of a scrapbook.

The festival name uses individual cutout letters with different typefaces, sizes and colours. Their uneven placement gives the lettering a handmade quality, with scraps of paper and tape behind it. A bear carrying a backpack and rolled sleeping mat provides a smaller logo that connects directly to the camping theme. In Photoshop, overlapping photographs form an imagined landscape of forests, mountains and waterfalls. Torn edges expose newspaper and coloured paper beneath the images, while grain and colour adjustments help the separate pieces feel like part of the same collage. The poster uses the same cutout lettering for Aurora, Pomme and Phoebe Bridgers, with the dates and location placed on cream strips of paper.

The Instagram carousel continues the landscape across three panels, placing artist photographs among the paper layers. Facebook and Twitter headers give more space to the festival name. Clothing and tote-bag mockups show the collage lettering on fabric, while the cup uses the simpler bear logo.

Solution

Wanderwood’s graphics give the festival a setting and a mood before the viewer reads the details. The woodland photographs suggest somewhere to explore, and the paper scraps make the artwork feel collected and personal. Artist portraits connect that setting to the music, while clear information strips keep the practical details readable. Across the different pieces, the recurring lettering, textures and colours make the festival easy to recognise without repeating the same layout.
